    How the score works

    This is a simple heuristic, not a standard. Quantum-vulnerable algorithms score 20 points to start. Add up to 40 for how far shelf life plus migration time overshoots your threat year, 15 for key exchange and encryption (harvest-now risk) or 5 to 10 for signatures (10 if hardware-bound), up to 15 for exposure, and up to 10 for how hard the asset is to replace. 70+ is critical, 50+ high, 30+ medium. AES-256, SHA-256 and the new ML-KEM, ML-DSA and SLH-DSA schemes score as no quantum risk. Adjust the weights to your own context.
